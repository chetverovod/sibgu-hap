## Description

Scenario with two HAP orbiters and two terminal groups.

- HAP #1: `satId=2`, traced by `ext-utils/circular.txt`
- HAP #2: `satId=3`, traced by `ext-utils/hap-500km-east.txt`
- Target separation between HAP centers is about 500 km.

Each HAP has its own nearby GW/UT group in `positions/gw_positions.txt`
and `positions/ut_positions.txt`.
